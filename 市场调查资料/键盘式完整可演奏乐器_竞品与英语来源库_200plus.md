## Design implications

- The most repeatable portable formula is **small keys + internal sound engine + battery/speaker + immediate sequencing or recording**.
- Step/pad keys save space, but need velocity, aftertouch, scale locking, chord memory or visual feedback to feel like an instrument rather than a controller.
- OP-1 field, MPC Key 37 and Organelle M represent polished closed workstation, production workstation and open programmable instrument respectively.
- Osmose, Hydrasynth Explorer, MicroFreak and Piano M make expressive sensing the primary product story.
- CT-S1000V and Melo-D suggest that AI/vocal-generation layers work best when deterministic note-level control remains available.
